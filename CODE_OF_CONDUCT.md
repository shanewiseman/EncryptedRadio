# Code of conduct

We welcome thoughtful, respectful participation. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces, including content produced by AI agents.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence; critique work without attacking people.
- Respect differing backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Accept constructive feedback, correct mistakes, and respect others' time.
- Respect privacy and consent. Do not publish another person's personal information.
- Take responsibility for content or actions produced by an agent acting on your behalf.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, sustained disruption, and doxxing are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer, [@shanewiseman](https://github.com/shanewiseman), using a contact method published on their profile. If none is available, request a private contact channel in an issue without including personal or sensitive details. Avoid posting the incident publicly while seeking a private channel.

The maintainer may remove or edit inappropriate content, request a correction, restrict participation, or ban participants depending on severity and repetition. Reports will be handled with care for the privacy and safety of those involved. Participants may ask the maintainer to reconsider an enforcement decision with relevant additional context.

This project currently has one designated maintainer and no independent appeals body.
